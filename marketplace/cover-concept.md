# Cover / logo concept (512 × 512)

Near-black background (`#0d1117`-like). Center: a vertical git graph of
three commit dots joined by a line, in signal green (`#2bd48a`). Next to
the top dot, a short header tag `feat:` in monospace white. A small
crossed-out sparkle (✦ with a slash) in muted slate at the bottom right
says "no AI signature". Flat shapes, no gradients, nothing under 48 px
so it reads at 64 px in the store grid. Light variant: graphite dots on
white.
